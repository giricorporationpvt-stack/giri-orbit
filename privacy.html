Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 26, 2026 • GIRI Corporation Pvt. Ltd.

Welcome to Giri Orbit ("we", "our", or "the Platform"), a sovereign productivity suite created by GIRI Corporation Pvt. Ltd. We are committed to safeguarding your privacy and ensuring you have complete control over your documents and personal information.

Sovereign Architecture Guarantee: Giri Orbit is built with client-side priority. Your documents, spreadsheets, presentations, and files are stored locally in your browser sandbox (IndexedDB and LocalStorage) or in your personal Google Drive account. We do not operate external database servers that harvest or store your private document content.

1. Information We Collect and Access

When you choose to use Giri Orbit with cloud synchronization (such as Google Drive Integration), we access only the minimal permissions required to provide cloud file storage functionality:

  • Google Account Information: Your name, email address, and profile picture provided via Google Identity Services solely to display your active account profile in the workspace interface.
  • Google Drive Files: Using the official https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope, Giri Orbit can only create, view, and modify files that were created by or opened with Giri Orbit. We cannot access unrelated files across your Google Drive.
  • Local Workspace Data: Documents created within Drift, Axis, Kinetic, and Aegis are saved in your local browser sandbox to enable full offline operation.

2. How We Use Information

The information accessed is utilized exclusively for:

  • Authenticating your session in the browser.
  • Saving, loading, and auto-syncing your documents to your own Google Drive.
  • Allowing seamless offline document editing with automatic reconnection.

We do NOT sell, rent, monetize, or transfer your personal data or document contents to any third parties or advertisers.

3. Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

Giri Orbit's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements:

  • We only request access to Google user data directly relevant to providing and improving file synchronization features.
  • We do not transfer Google user data to third parties unless necessary to comply with applicable laws or as part of a merger/acquisition.
  • We do not use Google user data to serve advertisements or train machine learning models.

4. Data Storage and Security

All authentication tokens and session credentials reside securely in your browser's private local storage. No intermediate server receives your Google credentials or unencrypted documents. All communication with Google Drive occurs over secure, encrypted HTTPS channels directly between your browser and Google servers.

5. User Control and Account Disconnection

You have complete control over your connected accounts at any time:

  • You can sign out of Google Drive directly in Giri Orbit by clicking Sign Out in the Drive modal.
  • You can permanently revoke Giri Orbit's access to your Google account anytime via Google Account Security Permissions.
  • Clearing your browser site data will remove all locally cached documents and session identifiers.

6. Developer & Data Protection Commitment

✓ Official GIRI Corporation Sovereign Commitment

GIRI Corporation Pvt. Ltd. makes the following binding commitments to every user of Giri Orbit:

  • Absolute Document Sovereignty: Your documents, spreadsheets, presentations, and files remain your exclusive property and are stored directly in your local browser sandbox or your personal Google Drive.
  • No Data Monetization: We do not sell, rent, license, or monetize your personal information or document contents under any circumstances.
  • No Third-Party Tracking: We do not share your Google profile or Drive files with third-party marketing brokers or advertising networks.
  • No AI Model Training on Private Data: We never utilize your private document content to train public artificial intelligence models without explicit, informed consent.
  • Compliance with Google Limited Use: Our use of Google API data strictly adheres to Google's Limited Use Requirements at all times.
